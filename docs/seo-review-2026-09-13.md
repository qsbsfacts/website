All guides were already reachable through the hub; the homepage now links directly to all twelve using their descriptive titles and three topic groups. This gives visitors and crawlers shorter paths without adding new articles or altering tax claims.

These are interaction events, not completed calculations or qualified leads. Neither sends field values, state selections, or amounts. Existing page URL/referrer query stripping remains in place.
