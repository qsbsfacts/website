# State guide verification — September 4, 2026

Four pages were written from primary state legislative and revenue sources. Each page has contextual links plus source-list props for the shared Guide layout. These are educational guides, not individualized residency determinations. No assumption that relocating is best or produces guaranteed tax savings is made.

## California QSBS

- [FTB 2025 Schedule D instructions](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-540-d-instructions.html) explicitly say California does not conform to IRC 1045 or 1202.
- [RTC §17014](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=RTC&sectionNum=17014.) establishes temporary/transitory residence and the employment-contract safe harbor: 546 uninterrupted days, disregarding returns totaling no more than 45 days per tax year, with >$200,000 intangible-income and principal-tax-avoidance-purpose exceptions. Failure of safe harbor does not replace the ordinary facts/circumstances analysis.
- [FTB Publication 1031, 2025](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-1031-publication.pdf) confirms conditions and residence factors. There is no universal 183-day escape rule.
- [RTC §17952](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=17952.&lawCode=RTC) supplies nonresident intangible-income business-situs/business-activity exceptions. Verified direct via urllib from the official site because web open returned a tool safety error.
- [FTB Publication 1004](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/misc/1004.html) distinguishes compensation allocation, subsequent stock gain, ISO qualifying/disqualifying dispositions, and AMT. Guide deliberately does not prescribe a single allocation period for all award types.

## California exit-tax page

- [FTB nonresident guidance](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/residency-status/part-year-and-nonresident.html) and [Publication 1100](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/misc/1100.html) describe income taxation after moving, not a blanket charge on departure. Installment outcomes depend on property and transaction facts; guide does not claim all later payments are taxable or exempt.
- [AB 259 official history](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billHistoryClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240AB259) shows February 1, 2024 filing with Chief Clerk under Joint Rule 56; bill not enacted.
- [SOS qualified ballot measures](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/ballot-measures/qualified-ballot-measures) currently lists Proposition 40 for November 3, 2026. [Official voter guide](https://voterguide.sos.ca.gov/propositions/40/index.htm) confirms proposed one-time wealth tax. Page explicitly labels pending, not enacted. Revisit after November 3, 2026. No claims about a future outcome or legal validity are made.

## Oregon

- [Enrolled SB 1507](https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2026R1/Downloads/MeasureDocument/SB1507): section 5 adds federally excluded IRC 1202 gain; section 10 applies it to tax years beginning January 1, 2026 or later. Text is not limited to post-2025-acquired stock or only incremental federal benefits. The guide marks the grandfathering conclusion as a reading of the enrolled text.
- [DOR 2026 summary](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/Pages/2026-summary-of-legislation.aspx) records Chapter 142, June 5, 2026 effective date. That date differs from tax-year applicability.
- [Governor signing letter, April 9, 2026](https://www.oregon.gov/gov/Documents/2026.04.09_SB%201507%20Signing%20Letter.pdf) commits to seeking legislation in 2027, not enacted restoration or a promised refund. Revisit during the 2027 session.
- [DOR residency](https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/individuals/pages/what-form.aspx) and [ORS 316](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors316.html) support domicile, more-than-200-day residence, part-year and source rules. Below 201 days is not a general domiciliary exemption.

- Cross-review resolved the stale referendum footnote on DOR summary: [official chapter law](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/lawsstatutes/2026orLaw0142.pdf), final note on printed p32, specifies only sections 2, 7, 9 (not QSBS section 5) and says petitioners failed to submit required signatures by June 4. Source and concise note added to Oregon page.

## New York

- Current [Tax Law §612](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/TAX/612), showing June 5, 2026 revision, starts from federal AGI and includes no general QSBS addback. Page explicitly presents conformity as a reading of current law, not a claimed DTF special ruling. A mention of §1202 in the qualified-investment provisions is not an exclusion reversal.
- [2026–27 enacted-budget index](https://www.assembly.state.ny.us/2026budget/?sec=enacted) linked so readers can distinguish proposals from final laws. No claim that every proposed budget provision was enacted.
- [DTF residency FAQ](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/file/nonresident-faqs.htm), [definitions](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/file/pit_definitions.htm), [§632](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/TAX/632), and [equity memo](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pdf/memos/income/m07_7i.pdf) support separate domicile/statutory residency, source rules, and compensation allocation. A fully federally excluded gain generally already excluded by NY does not gain a second exclusion from a move.
