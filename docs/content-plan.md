Three sub-agents research and author separate groups of four guides. The primary agent owns the shared guide layout, index, navigation, links from existing pages, and validation.

OpenSEO research in this conversation informed priorities: qualified small business stock (1,900 estimated monthly US searches); QSBS requirements (1,300); QSBS eligibility (480); holding period and limit (140 each); new QSBS rules (110); California QSBS (90); New York QSBS (70). These are third-party keyword estimates, overlap, and are not traffic forecasts. Broader California exit-tax searches require a careful residency explainer, not claims of guaranteed tax savings.

Editorial requirements: consult current primary sources; link material claims; distinguish issued/acquired dates, enacted law/proposals, exclusion/deferral, compensation/gains, and federal/state treatment. Examples state their assumptions. The guides do not establish an individual's eligibility, tax residency, or best transaction strategy.

Acceptance: all 20 routes build, all existing calculator tests pass, metadata is unique and concise, canonicals agree with sitemap URLs, internal links/fragments resolve, every page is reachable from the homepage, and representative guides work in a browser at desktop and mobile widths.
